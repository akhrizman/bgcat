About Us

Modern board games have evolved far beyond Monopoly and Scrabble. Today there are thousands of engaging games that encourage teamwork, strategy, creativity, communication, and laughter for every age and experience level. Our motto at The Board Game Catalyst is "There's a board game for everyone!" Let us share our passion for board games with you and your group.

How does it work? A host from The Board Game Catalyst will bring a carefully curated selection of games directly to you. Every event is customized to your group, whether you're looking for a screen-free social activity, a team-building experience, or an ongoing game night. If your group is looking to build connection through modern board games, or just curious about what the hobby has to offer, we're here to make it happen.

We handle everything, from selecting the right games and bringing the materials to teaching the rules so participants can focus on having fun. We strive to create a memorable experience by making your gaming event stress-free. No previous gaming experience is required.

What We Offer

Learn & Play: Want to get your group into modern board games but not sure how to get started? Over one or more sessions, we will teach a new game or two per session and be there to help with rules clarifications and strategy advice. Let us choose the line-up from a tried-and-true sampling of "gateway" games or tell us if you have specific ones in mind. After a few sessions, you'll be ready to explore new titles on your own!

Club Kickstart: Let us help you start up a club at your library, school, or center. We'll bring a variety of games and teach them on-the-fly. This can be a one-and-done or recurring event until enough people in your group have built up their own collection of games. Then your attendees can take over the role of teaching.

The Teach: Have a specific game you've always wanted to learn or tried to learn but got thwarted by a confusing rulebook? Look no further, get a group together and we'll take care of the rest. Most likely, we'll even have the game in our collection.

Teacher Training: Geared to those who already have a passion for the hobby. Do you get frustrated when teaching games? Do the people you're teaching get frustrated? Constant interruptions? Strategy questions? Everyone learns differently. Let our experienced game instructors show you the tips and tricks to refine your "teach" so people keep coming back to you for more games!

Grown-Up Get-Togethers Having a casual get-together with friends and want some lite entertainment? How about Just One or Codenames? Telestrations or Wits & Wagers? Or maybe Cash & Guns or Good Cop Bad Cop? If you've never heard of these, you're missing out! You supply the drinks, we'll bring the games and get your guests laughing, drawing, and showing off their creative sides.

Team Building We specialize in collaborative "Cooperative Games" and Escape Room Games that will get your group working together for the greater good (or bad in some cases). If you want the experience of winning or losing as a team or want your employees to have that experience in a way they will actually enjoy, let us host your next office game night!

Youngsters (5+) Want to trick your kids into doing math, reading, & logic under the guise of gaming? Our wide selection of games offer youngsters true autonomy, where their choices determine their outcomes. For younger players we suggest cooperative games as a great non-competitive option. Can you get all the owls home before night time by choosing the most optimal cards to play? Can you place the right tile at the right time to collect the most baby dragons? Let's find out together!
