About

Modern board games have evolved far beyond Monopoly and Scrabble. Today there are thousands of engaging games that encourage teamwork, strategy, creativity, communication, and laughter for every age and experience level. Our motto at the Board Game Catalyst is "There's a board game for everyone!"

Our mission is in our name and modern board and card games are our focus. Whether they be casual party games or complex strategy games, we'll introduce you and your group to games you'll love but never knew existed.

A host from The Board Game Catalyst will bring a carefully curated selection of games directly to you. Every event is customized to your group, whether you're looking for a screen-free social activity, a team-building experience, or an ongoing game night. If your group is looking to build connection through modern board games, or just curious about what the hobby has to offer, we're here to make it happen.

We handle everything, from selecting the right games and bringing the materials to teaching the rules so participants can focus on having fun. We strive to create a memorable experience by making your gaming event stress-free. No previous gaming experience is required.
